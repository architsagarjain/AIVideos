## EXPLORING SAFER TOMORROWS AT OSH INDIA 2026

A glimpse of **Team Meghdoot at OSH India, Goregaon, Mumbai**, exploring the latest developments in occupational safety, health, and workplace practices.

Representing Meghdoot Logistics & Infra at the exhibition were **Rahul Purohit, Raju Sahu, Suresh Sharma, and Dayanand Dhali**.

For an industry where heavy lifting, specialized transportation, and work at height are part of everyday operations, staying informed about evolving safety practices and technologies is essential.

The exhibition provided an opportunity to explore new ideas, connect with industry professionals, and gain insights that can contribute to safer and more efficient project execution.

**Because safety is not just a priority on site. It is a commitment we continue to strengthen.**
